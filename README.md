📌 Project Overview

WAIN is an AI-powered place recommendation agent designed to help users discover and choose places, activities, and restaurants in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia based on their preferences.

❗️ Problem Statement

Finding a suitable place often requires searching across multiple websites and applications. This can make it difficult and time-consuming to:

Find places within a specific budget
Discover activities that match personal interests
Find places in a preferred location
Compare different options
Make quick decisions based on multiple preferences

💡 Our Solution

WAIN is an AI-powered recommendation agent designed specifically for Riyadh.

Users can describe what they are looking for in natural language, and WAIN processes their preferences to filter, rank, and recommend relevant places from structured data.

This allows users to explore suitable options based on criteria such as:

💰 Budget
📍 Location
⭐️ Rating
🍽️ Category
👨‍👩‍👧 Family-friendly preferences
🕐 Opening hours
